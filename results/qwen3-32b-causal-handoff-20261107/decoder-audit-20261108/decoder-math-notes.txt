Confirmed behavior, not a confirmed production defect:
- Every one of the eight leave-template-out training folds has 224 rows and encoding design A=[intercept, own, source, names, order, incoming] with A.T@A=224 I. Thus the normal-equation condition number is exactly 1; the encoding contrast is a scalar shrinkage of mean(y*states), or half the class mean difference. The nuisance coefficients and incoming contrast are not confounded in this fully crossed design.
- The contrast is projected into the lens span, normalized, and followed by a scalar regression. It is not a general discriminative linear decoder, even for residual_contrast with no span restriction. Noise covariance can make the class mean difference a poor discriminant even when another linear direction is perfect.
- Exact diagnostic counterexample used all 256 declared development rows, all eight original folds, ridge=1e-6 and identity 2D lens: q=source_sign*order_sign; states=[y+10q,10q]. Current fit_component returns basis=[1,0], incoming_cv=.5, own_cv=.5, incoming_basis_own_cv=.5 and incoming_basis_nuisance_cv=.5. A training-only discriminative ridge probe with fixed alpha=1 gets 1.0 CV in the same folds, as does explicit w=[1,-1]. Its fitted weights in every fold are [.9911506383,-.9911063924]. No threshold or test template tuning was used. Main-effect nuisance adjustment does not remove source*order interactions.
- Existing test_conditional_decoder_template_folds_control_own_and_nuisance uses perfectly orthogonal, zero-offset synthetic coordinates. It establishes basic signal extraction and fold disjointness; it cannot establish robustness to correlated features, anisotropy, interactions, template shifts or actual hidden-state numerics.

Scalar ridge details:
- _ridge penalizes the intercept as well as slope. With balanced training labels, scalar decision threshold is mean(x)*n/(n+ridge). A centered solver retaining this penalty is mathematically equivalent; merely centering and leaving intercept unpenalized changes the estimator.
- Toy x=100 + 1e-8*y yields 50% accuracy with both original and exact-centered same-penalty implementation; unpenalized-intercept centered gives 100%. x=1e8 + .001*y behaves likewise. These are translation sensitivity of the penalty, not evidence of floating point failure.
- Available actual attempt-003 control traces (32 rows) evaluated with attempt-004 full-data incoming bases have maximum absolute coordinate mean 105.4313, minimum coordinate std .1214 and maximum raw scalar Gram condition 796633.9. At those means, intercept penalty threshold shift is under 5e-7; this does not support catastrophic numeric failure. These controls are not the 256 development traces and do not substitute for an actual-fold reproduction.
